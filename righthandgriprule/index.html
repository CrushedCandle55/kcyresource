How to use the Right Hand Grip Rule

1. Grip the wire or coil with your right hand.
2. Point your thumb in the direction of the conventional current (from + to −).
3. The direction in which your fingers curl shows the direction of the magnetic field lines.

For a solenoid: fingers follow the current around the coils, thumb points to the North pole of the solenoid.
